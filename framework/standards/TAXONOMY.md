# Repository Taxonomy

**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0.0

- **Program:** a durable coordinated portfolio with shared outcomes.
- **Project:** a bounded delivery effort with an owner and completion signal.
- **Domain:** a stable subject or practice area shared across projects.
- **Research area:** the narrower topic under investigation.
- **Mission:** an authorized, bounded unit of work.
- **Profile:** domain- or mode-specific rules extending the Praxis kernel.

Artifacts remain organized by type and carry project, domain, and research-area
metadata. Generated views provide alternate navigation. Shared evidence is
stored once and referenced by every applicable project.

Kernel rules cover identity, provenance, lifecycle, supersession, confidence,
canonical/generated separation, validation, and handoff. Domain profiles may
add stricter evidence, safety, or review requirements but may not weaken the
kernel.
