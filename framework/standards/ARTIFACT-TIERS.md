# Artifact Tier Standard

**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0.0

Use the smallest tier that preserves traceability.

## Research note

For one bounded observation or decision with no multi-step investigation.
Create the relevant evidence, decision, or concept record. A full REP is not
required.

## Research cycle

For a meaningful investigation that tests hypotheses. Requires a journal plus
the evidence, hypothesis, experiment, or theory records actually affected.
End with a concise handoff in the journal or mission.

## Full REP

Required for major synthesis, cross-session or cross-agent handoff, theory
change, material decision support, or research whose reconstruction matters.
It follows the canonical REP specification.

Mission metadata declares `artifact_tier`. Risk or consequence may raise the
tier. Boilerplate does not substitute for evidence.
