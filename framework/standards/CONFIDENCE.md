# Confidence Standard

**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0.0

Confidence describes evidential support, not lifecycle maturity or work
completion.

| Label | Optional estimate | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `very-low` | 0.00-0.19 | Speculative; little reliable support |
| `low` | 0.20-0.39 | Limited support or major unresolved contradictions |
| `medium` | 0.40-0.69 | Meaningful support with material uncertainty |
| `high` | 0.70-0.89 | Strong, diverse support; bounded residual uncertainty |
| `very-high` | 0.90-1.00 | Exceptional support within a clearly stated scope |

Numeric estimates are optional judgments unless a documented method gives
them statistical meaning. Every accepted artifact with confidence should state
the rationale. Confidence may rise or fall without changing lifecycle state.
